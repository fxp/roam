SLOT C1 · WHO'S IN CHARGE

Autonomous, not unaccountable.

You write the rules once. The agent operates inside them — and four mechanisms keep it on the leash:

Your rules SET ONCE, EDIT ANYTIME

Price-change caps, locked slots, spending limits, your available hours, hard no-gos. Every agent action is checked against them — by code, not by the AI's good intentions.

Probation FIRST 14 DAYS

Early on, every product swap and price move waits 24 hours for your eyes before taking effect. Stop intervening, and the agent earns full autonomy.

The decision log EVERY ACTION

Each move records what it did, what it knew, and why. Two taps from any activity line to the full reasoning. Nothing is a black box.

The kill switch ALWAYS TWO TAPS AWAY

Freeze everything — swaps, prices, customer replies — in under five seconds, from any screen. Money and signatures were never the agent's to touch.

SLOT D1 · STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Asked by every operator so far.

Actually autonomous, or approval hell?

Inside your rules, it decides without asking — what to stock, what to price, when you restock, what to tell customers. It comes to you for three things: the run itself, the deals, and calls above your caps. Our benchmark: ignore the app for two weeks and your machines keep selling the right things.

Do my customers know they're chatting with an AI?

Yes, always. The QR chat introduces itself as the machine's AI operator up front. It only handles machine and product topics, never collects personal data beyond an opt-in restock alert, and anything it can't handle escalates straight to you.

What if it stocks the wrong thing?

Worst case is a slow slot for a week — not a disaster. Every decision is logged with its reasoning, next week's sales data corrects it, and you can veto or lock any slot at any time. Money and contracts were never delegated, so the blast radius stays small.

I don't own any machines yet. Too early?

Honestly — Vendling shines once machines are in the field. If you're at zero, it preps your first deal (location one-pagers, ROI math, a ready contract), you close it, and the agent takes over the moment the machine is plugged in.

How is this different from vending management software?

Management software shows you dashboards; the deciding and planning is still your job. Vendling decides, plans, talks to your customers, and hands you a finished run card. You don't read charts — you drive.
